This guide helps you pick the right registration setup for your event and shows you how to manage registrations effectively.

## Choosing Your Registration Mode

### Decision Tree

Ask yourself these questions:

**1. Should anyone be able to find your tournament?**
- **Yes** → Public (Open or Approval Required)
- **No** → Private (Invite Only or Invite with Approval)

**2. Do you want to vet teams before confirming?**
- **Yes** → Approval Required or Invite with Approval
- **No** → Open or Invite Only

| Your situation | Best mode |
|----------------|-----------|
| Weekly community scrim, everyone welcome | **Open** |
| Competitive tournament, want to check teams | **Approval Required** |
| Private practice with specific teams | **Invite Only** |
| Paid event — collect payment, then approve | **Invite with Approval** |

## Setting Up Requirements

During tournament creation (Step 1: Tournament Details), toggle the requirements you want:

### Requirement Combinations

| Goal | Toggle these | What players experience |
|------|-------------|----------------------|
| Build your club community | **Require Club Membership** | Registrant prompted to join your club |
| Ensure complete teams | **Require Full Roster** | Team must fill all slots before registration is complete |
| Full club community + complete teams | **Require All Members in Club** | All members join club + fill roster (auto-enables first two) |
| Track individual player performance | **Require Game UID & IGN** | Players provide Free Fire UID and IGN for this tournament |
| Maximum accountability | All four toggled on | Full control — club membership, roster, and identity verification |

> **Tip:** For casual scrims, skip all requirements — keep registration frictionless. For competitive events, enable UID/IGN at minimum so you can track per-player kills and use Smart Results.

### UID/IGN Submission Modes

When you enable "Require Game UID & IGN", how UIDs get submitted depends on whether you also enable "Require Full Roster":

- **With Full Roster:** Each team member provides their own UID and IGN individually from the Registration Status page
- **Without Full Roster:** The registrant submits UIDs and IGNs for all team members in one form

Both modes work — choose based on whether you want individual accountability (full roster) or faster registration (registrant handles it).

## Managing Registrations

### The Registrations Dashboard

<Screenshot src={ss_registrations_page} alt="Registrations page" caption="Filter, search, and manage all registrations from one page" />

From the Tournament Dashboard **Registrations** tab, you can:

1. **Filter by status** — All, Pending, Confirmed, Applied, Rejected
2. **Search** by name, squad name, or ID
3. **Click any registration** to open the detail panel

### Reviewing a Registration

<Screenshot src={ss_registrations_detail_panel} alt="Registration detail panel" caption="Team info, members, requirement progress, and actions" />

The detail panel shows:
- Team name and registrant
- All members with their details
- Requirement progress (which are complete, which need action)
- Approve, Reject, or Delete buttons

### Bulk Actions for Large Tournaments

<Screenshot src={ss_registrations_bulk_actions} alt="Bulk actions" caption="Select multiple registrations and approve, reject, or delete in bulk" />

For tournaments with 50+ registrations:

1. Filter to **Pending** registrations
2. Select all (or pick specific ones)
3. Click **Bulk Approve**, **Bulk Reject**, or **Bulk Delete**

> **Tip:** After your registration deadline, filter to Pending, review the list, then bulk approve all valid teams and bulk reject the rest. Much faster than one-by-one.

## Common Setups

### Casual Weekly Scrim
- Mode: **Open**
- Requirements: None
- Result: Players register and are confirmed instantly. Minimal friction.

### Community Tournament (50-100 teams)
- Mode: **Approval Required**
- Requirements: **Require Club Membership** + **Require Game UID & IGN**
- Result: Teams apply, you vet them, they join your club. UID enables kill tracking.

### Competitive Open (100+ teams)
- Mode: **Approval Required**
- Requirements: **Require All Members in Club** + **Require Game UID & IGN**
- Result: Maximum accountability. All players are in your club, all have verified UIDs.

### Paid Invitational
- Mode: **Invite with Approval**
- Requirements: **Require Game UID & IGN**
- Result: Share invite link after payment. Approve after verifying payment. UIDs for kill tracking.
