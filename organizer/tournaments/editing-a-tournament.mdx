You can edit your tournament after creating it. From the Tournament Dashboard, click **Edit** to open the editor.

## Layout

The edit page uses expandable sections (not the same wizard as creation). You'll see four sections:

- **Tournament Details** — name, description, poster, format, visibility, requirements
- **Schedule** — registration and tournament dates
- **Game Settings** — scoring, platforms, team size
- **Communication** — Discord and WhatsApp links

Open any section to make changes.

## Field Restrictions

Some fields become locked as your tournament progresses. Locked fields show a colored badge:

- **Red badge** — field is fully locked and can't be changed
- **Amber badge** — field has a timing restriction (can only be extended or delayed, not shortened)

Hover over the badge to see why the field is locked.

### What gets locked and when:

- **After publishing:** Format, game mode, visibility, slots, and team size — changing these would confuse players who've already seen the tournament.
- **After registration opens:** Registration mode, club membership requirements, and UID/IGN settings — changing requirements mid-registration would be unfair.
- **After tournament goes Live:** Scoring settings (kill points, placement points, tiebreaker) — changing scoring mid-tournament would invalidate existing results.
- **Communication links** (WhatsApp, Discord) are always editable, regardless of status.

### Date restrictions after publishing:

- **Registration Opens** can only be delayed (moved later), not moved earlier
- **Tournament Ends** can only be extended (moved later), not shortened

This prevents confusing players who've already planned around your schedule.

## Draft Tournaments

If your tournament is still in Draft status, everything is editable. You'll see a banner with the option to publish directly from the edit page.

> **Tip:** Take your time editing while in Draft — once you publish, some fields get locked permanently. Get format, game mode, and slots right before publishing.
