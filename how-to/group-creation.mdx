This guide walks you through setting up groups for a tournament stage — from configuration to locking.

## Before You Start

You need:
- A published roadmap with at least one stage
- Registrations confirmed (or invited slots filled)

## Step 1: Open Group Configuration

From your stage's manage page, go to **Groups**. If no groups exist, click **Configure auto-create**.

<Screenshot src={ss_auto_groups_config} alt="Auto-groups configuration" caption="Set naming, seeding, and fill strategies before generating groups" />

## Step 2: Configure Group Settings

### Group Naming

Choose how groups are named:

- **Auto-generate names** — set a prefix (e.g., "Group") and pattern (Letters A-Z or Numbers 1-9). Result: "Group A", "Group B", etc.
- **Custom names** — name each group yourself (e.g., "Warriors Pool", "Legends Pool")

> **Tip:** Use auto-generated names for qualifier stages (Group A, B, C...) and custom names for branded final stages.

### Invited Slots Distribution

If your stage has invited slots, choose how they're spread across groups:

| Strategy | What happens | Best for |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Distribute evenly** | Invited teams spread across all groups | Fair competition |
| **Concentrate** | Pack invited teams into early groups | Keeping them together |
| **Seed into first groups** | Place invited teams in Group A first, then B, etc. | Priority seeding |

### Seeding Strategy

How registered teams are distributed:

- **Balanced random** — shuffle teams while keeping groups even-sized. Works for most events.
- **Rank-based** — distribute by skill tier (coming soon)

### Fill Strategy

How remaining slots are filled after invited teams are placed:

- **Balance across all groups** — every group gets roughly the same number of teams
- **Fill one group at a time** — fill Group A completely, then Group B, etc.

> **Tip:** "Balance across all groups" is the right choice 90% of the time. Use "Fill one at a time" only when you want some groups to be full and others partially filled.

## Step 3: Preview and Create

1. Click **Preview Groups** to see how teams would be distributed
2. Review the preview — check group sizes and which teams are where

<Screenshot src={ss_auto_groups_preview} alt="Group preview" caption="Preview your groups before creating — check sizes and team placement" />

3. If the preview looks good, click **Create Groups**
4. A progress animation shows the groups being built

> **Tip:** Always preview first. If the distribution doesn't look right, adjust your seeding or fill strategy and preview again.

## Step 4: Review and Adjust

After groups are created, you can:

### Move Teams Between Groups

Select one or more teams, then use the **Multi Swap** view to move them to a different group.

<Screenshot src={ss_groups_multi_swap} alt="Multi Swap view" caption="Select teams and move them between groups" />

> **Tip:** Use Multi Swap to manually balance groups if the auto-distribution put too many strong teams in one group.

### Enable Auto-Assign (Optional)

Toggle **Enable auto-assign** if registrations are still coming in. The system automatically distributes new registrations into groups every 15 minutes, using your fill and seeding strategies.

Useful for large tournaments where registrations arrive over days or weeks.

## Step 5: Lock Groups

Before you can create matches, groups must be locked:

<Screenshot src={ss_groups_page_locked} alt="Locked groups" caption="Green indicator = locked, amber = unlocked" />

Locking means:
- No more moving teams between groups
- Auto-assign stops
- Group composition is finalized
- You can now create matches

Each group shows a lock status indicator (green = locked, amber = unlocked).

### Need to Make Changes After Locking?

Use **Unlock Groups** at the top of the page. Only groups whose matches haven't started can be unlocked.

> **Tip:** Do a final review before locking: check that invited slots are filled, team counts are balanced, and you're happy with the distribution. Once you create matches, unlocking becomes more disruptive.

## Common Group Setups

### 192-Team Qualifier (Squad)
- 16 groups of 12 squads
- Balanced random seeding
- Balance fill across all groups
- 0 invited slots

### 64-Team Semifinals (Squad)
- 8 groups of 8 squads
- Balanced random seeding
- Balance fill
- Advancing teams from previous stage auto-registered

### 24-Team Finals with Invited Pros (Squad)
- 2 groups of 12 squads
- 6 invited slots distributed evenly (3 per group)
- Balanced random seeding for remaining 18 spots
