## Profile

Your profile is your public identity on SquadsSpace. Access it from the navigation.

{/* TODO: screenshot — Profile page */}

Your profile page shows:

- **Hero banner** — your display name, username, and avatar
- **Player details** — your basic information
- **Tournaments** — tournaments you've participated in
- **Social links** — connected social accounts
- **Clubs** — clubs you've joined

> **Tip:** Keep your display name recognizable — it's what organizers and teammates see in registrations and leaderboards.

## Settings

Settings gives you control over your account. It has six tabs:

### General

A quick-start checklist with shortcuts to the most important setup steps — completing your profile, securing your account, and reviewing active sessions.

### Account

Manage your core account details — display name, email, and other identity information.

### Notifications

Control which notifications you receive. You can customize notifications globally or per tournament:

- Toggle notification types on or off (match scheduling, results, room details, announcements)
- Set preferences for specific tournaments or groups
- Changes take effect immediately

### Security

Manage your password, enable two-factor authentication, and set up passkeys for faster sign-in.

> **Tip:** Enable two-factor authentication for extra security — especially if you're an organizer managing tournaments.

### Sessions

View and manage your active login sessions. See where you're signed in and revoke access to any session you don't recognize.

### Privacy

Control your privacy preferences and data visibility.
