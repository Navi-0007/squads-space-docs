The Organizer Dashboard is the first screen you see in the organizer view. Access it from the sidebar or by navigating to the Organizer section.

## Club Details

Shows your club's name, owner, member count, creation date, and region. This is the same information players see on your public club page.

## Quick Actions

Shortcut cards to the most common tasks:

- **Create Tournament** — start a new tournament or scrim
- **View Tournaments** — see all your events
- **Manage Members** — view and manage club members
- **Live Scoring** — start a live scoring session for streaming
- **Smart Results** — upload screenshots to enter scores quickly

> **Tip:** Use Smart Results from here to jump straight into uploading a screenshot — great when you want to enter scores without navigating through tournament pages.

## Tournaments

A list of your tournaments showing current status, format, and registration stats. Click any tournament to go directly to its Tournament Dashboard.

## Social Links

Shows the social links you've configured for your club — Discord, YouTube, and WhatsApp. These appear on your public club page and help players connect with your community.

> **Tip:** Keep your Discord and WhatsApp links up to date — players check these when they register for your events. Manage them from Club Settings.

## Sidebar Navigation

From any organizer page, the sidebar gives you quick access to:

- **Dashboard** — this page
- **Club Settings** — edit club name, region, social links, and banner
- **Members** — view and manage club members
- **Live Scoring** — standalone scoring sessions
- **Tournaments** — full tournament list with creation and management
