## Step 1: Tournament Details

From your Organizer Dashboard, go to **Tournaments** and click **Create**. Choose **Tournament** as the event type.

This first step covers everything about your tournament setup:

### Name, Description, and Poster

- **Tournament name** — what players will see (e.g., "Phoenix Cup Season 3")
- **Description** — rules, prizes, schedule, or anything players need to know
- **Poster** — upload an image for your tournament (PNG, JPG, or WEBP — recommended 1600×900)

> **Tip:** A good poster makes your tournament stand out in listings. Use a clear, high-quality image.

### Prize Pool (Optional)

If your tournament has a prize pool, toggle it on and configure:

- **Prize type** — Cash, Diamonds, or Mixed
- **Amount** — the prize value and currency

### Format and Settings

- **Tournament Format** — Solo, Duo, or Squad
- **Game Mode** — Battle Royale or Clash Squad
- **Tournament Type** — Scrim, Tournament, or Major
- **Visibility** — Public or Private
- **Registration Mode** — Open, Approval Required, Invite Only, or Invite with Approval
- **Slots** — how many teams can register

See [Registration Modes](/organizer/tournaments/registration-modes) for details on when to use each.

### Schedule

Set your tournament timeline:

- **Registration Opens** — when players can begin registering
- **Registration Closes** — when registration ends
- **Tournament Starts** — when matches begin
- **Tournament Ends** — when the event ends (optional)

> **Tip:** Leave at least a day between registration close and tournament start. This gives you time to set up groups, review registrations, and handle any issues.

### Requirements

Toggle the requirements players must meet to register:

- **Require Club Membership** — the registering player must be a member of your club
- **Require Full Roster** — the team must have the minimum number of members filled (e.g., 4 for Squad)
- **Require All Members in Club** — every team member must be on the platform and a member of your club. This auto-enables the first two requirements.
- **Require Game UID & IGN** — players must provide their Free Fire User ID and In-Game Name for this tournament. You can enable this with or without requiring a full roster.

See [Registration Requirements](/organizer/tournaments/requirements) for when to use each.

### Communication Links

- **Discord Server** — your Discord server invite link (optional)
- **WhatsApp Group** — your WhatsApp group link (optional)

## Step 2: Game Configuration

Configure your scoring and platform settings:

### Scoring System

- **Points per Kill** — how many points per kill (e.g., 1 kill = 1 point)
- **Tiebreaker Priority** — Placement First or Kills First
- **Placement Points** — points for each finishing position (1st through 12th for Squad, up to 48th for Solo)

> **Tip:** Most Indian Free Fire tournaments use 1 point per kill. For placement, a common setup is: 1st = 12 points, 2nd = 9, 3rd = 8, scaling down to 12th = 0.

### Allowed Platforms

Select which platforms are allowed — Android, iOS, or PC. You can allow multiple.

### Team Size

Team size is automatically set based on your format (Solo = 1, Duo = 2, Squad = 4-6). This is display-only — you can't change it separately.

## Step 3: Review & Create

Review all your settings before creating. Everything is displayed in a summary view so you can double-check before confirming.

## Step 4: Complete

Your tournament is created and starts in **Draft** status. From here it moves through these states:

- **Draft** — created but not visible to players
- **Published** — visible to players, registration hasn't started yet
- **Registration Open** — registration period is active
- **Registration Closed** — registration ended, no more signups
- **Live** — tournament has started
- **Completed** — tournament finished
- **Paused** — temporarily paused
- **Cancelled** — tournament cancelled

You can reopen registration after it closes. If the start date has already passed, you'll need to set a new start date.
