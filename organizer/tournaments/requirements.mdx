---
title: "Registration Requirements"
description: "Configure what players must meet before their registration is complete."
---

When creating a tournament or scrim, you can toggle up to four requirements that players must satisfy to register. These apply regardless of your registration mode.

## The 4 Requirements

### 1. Require Club Membership

The player submitting the registration must be a member of your club. If they're not already a member, they'll be prompted to join your club during the registration process. This only applies to the player who is registering the team — not all team members.

### 2. Require Full Roster

The team must have the minimum number of members filled based on the game mode:

- **Solo** — 1 member
- **Duo** — 2 members
- **Squad** — 4 members (up to 6 with substitutes)

The registration won't be considered complete until the roster meets this requirement.

### 3. Require All Members in Club

Every team member must be registered on the platform AND be a member of your club. This automatically enables the first two requirements (club membership + full roster).

The registering player is prompted to join the club at the time of registration. Other team members will see a banner on the status page telling them to join the club, along with details on which members still need to join.

### 4. Require Game UID & IGN

Players must provide their Free Fire User ID and In-Game Name for this tournament.

This is set per tournament — players enter their UID and IGN for each event. This requirement is **independent** — you can enable it with or without requiring a full roster.

**How UIDs get submitted depends on your setup:**

- **With full roster enabled:** Each team member provides their own UID and IGN individually from the Registration Status page.
- **Without full roster:** The person who registered the team submits UIDs and IGNs for all members together.

> **Tip:** Enable UID/IGN for any tournament where you want per-player kill tracking or plan to use Smart Results. It lets the system match scores to individual players automatically.

## How Requirements Work Together

Only one requirement auto-enables others:

- **Require All Members in Club** → auto-enables Require Club Membership + Require Full Roster
- **Require Game UID & IGN** → does NOT auto-enable anything. It works independently.

## What Players See

Players see all requirements listed on the registration page before they register. After registering, the status page shows:

- Their registration status
- A progress tracker for each requirement — which are complete, which still need action
- A banner at the top if action is needed (e.g., "Join club" or "Provide UID")

## When to Use What

| Scenario | Recommended Requirements |
|----------|------------------------|
| Casual weekly scrim | None — keep it frictionless |
| Community tournament | Require Club Membership |
| Competitive tournament | Require Full Roster + Require Game UID & IGN |
| Serious invitational | All four — full control |
