---
title: "UID/IGN Verification"
description: "Why and when to require Free Fire UID and IGN from players."
---

UID/IGN verification is an optional requirement you can enable when creating a tournament or scrim. When enabled, players must provide their Free Fire User ID (UID) and In-Game Name (IGN) for that specific tournament.

## What It Enables

### Per-Player Kill Tracking

With verified UIDs, organizers can enter kills per individual player — not just team totals. This means:

- Individual kill leaderboards alongside team standings
- MVP identification — easily spot the top fraggers in your tournament
- Dispute resolution — verified kill counts tied to real identities
- Player scouting — organizers and teams can spot talent based on actual performance data

### Smart Results

When UID/IGN is enabled, Smart Results can automatically match scores to individual players. Upload a screenshot of the results screen, and the system identifies each player by their UID — no manual matching needed.

> **Tip:** Smart Results works best when UID/IGN is enabled. Without it, you can still upload screenshots, but scores are matched to teams only, not individual players.

### Anti-Fraud

- Prevents unauthorized substitutions — players can't swap in without the organizer knowing
- Reduces fake registrations — verified identities add accountability
- Cleaner competitive environment overall

## How UIDs Get Submitted

UID/IGN is collected per tournament — players provide it for each event, not once globally. How it's submitted depends on your tournament setup:

**With "Require Full Roster" enabled:**
- Each team member provides their own UID and IGN individually
- They do this from the Registration Status page after registering
- A progress tracker shows which members have submitted and which haven't

**Without "Require Full Roster":**
- The person who registered the team submits UIDs and IGNs for all members together
- They enter team member details in one form on the Registration Status page

Players can also load a UID from their profile if they've saved one before — saving time across multiple tournaments.

## When to Use It

| Scenario | UID/IGN Verification |
|----------|---------------------|
| Casual weekly scrim | Skip it — keep registration fast |
| Competitive tournament | Enable it — per-player tracking and dispute prevention |
| Prize pool event | Definitely enable — you need accountability |
| Practice lobby with friends | Skip it — unnecessary friction |

> **Tip:** You can enable UID/IGN with or without requiring a full roster. For scrims where you want kill tracking but don't need full teams, enable UID alone.

## Where Organizers See UIDs

UID and IGN data appears in the registration detail panel. You'll see:

- The registering player's UID and IGN
- All team members' UIDs and IGNs (with status showing who submitted)
- This data is also used in slot lists and score entry to identify players
