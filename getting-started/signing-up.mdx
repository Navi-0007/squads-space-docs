## Creating an Account

1. Go to [platform.squads-space.com](https://platform.squads-space.com/auth)
2. Enter your email address
3. Set a password and fill in your display name and username
4. Verify your email with the code sent to your inbox
5. You're in — the dashboard loads automatically

> **Tip:** Check your spam or promotions folder if you don't see the verification email within a minute.

> **Tip:** Choose your username carefully — it's visible on your profile and in tournament registrations. You can change it later from Settings.

## Signing In

1. Go to [platform.squads-space.com](https://platform.squads-space.com/auth)
2. Enter your email and password
3. If you have two-factor authentication enabled, enter the code from your authenticator app

## Passkeys

SquadsSpace supports passkeys for faster, passwordless sign-in. If your device supports it, you can set one up from **Settings → Security**.

> **Tip:** Passkeys work with fingerprint, face unlock, or your device PIN — no password needed. Great for quick access on mobile.

## Forgot Password

Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in screen. You'll receive a reset link by email.

## What's Next

After signing in, you'll land on your Player Dashboard. From there:

- **Browse tournaments** — find events to join
- **Set up your squad** — create or join a team
- **Become an organizer** — open a ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/ySAsxatCwv) and we'll get you set up
