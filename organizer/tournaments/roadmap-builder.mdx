The Roadmap Builder is where you define your tournament's stages and how teams progress through them.

## Getting Started

From your Tournament Dashboard, go to the **Roadmap** tab. If no roadmap exists yet, click **Configure** to open the builder.

## Building Your Roadmap

For each stage you add, configure:

- **Entrants** — total teams in this stage (auto-calculated for later rounds based on qualifiers + invited slots)
- **Invited Slots** — extra squads you manually add to this round, on top of qualifying squads. Use for partner teams, wildcard entries, or defending champions.
- **Group Size** — how many teams per group (based on game mode — e.g., 12 for Squad)
- **Group Count** — auto-calculated based on entrants and group size
- **Qualifiers per Group** — how many teams advance from each group
- **Matches per Group** — how many matches each group plays
- **Final Round** — check this for the last stage (limits to 1 group, requires a winner count)

The system calculates totals automatically — group counts, how many need to advance from prior stages to fill remaining slots, etc.

## Stage Types

### Elimination

Teams are divided into groups, and matches happen within each group. Top performers advance to the next stage.

- Each group contains teams based on the game mode (e.g., 12 squads per group)
- Each group plays up to 8 matches
- Points accumulate across matches within the group
- Top teams from each group advance to the next stage

**Best for:** Early stages where you need to narrow down a large number of teams quickly.

> **Example:** Open Qualifiers — 192 squads in 16 groups of 12. Each group plays 4 matches. Top 4 from each group advance to Semifinals.

### Round Robin

Teams are split into groups, but unlike Elimination, matches happen in pairings — not within individual groups. Each group plays every other group once.

- Groups are created first (e.g., 3 groups of 6 squads)
- Pairings are configured after the roadmap is published, from the stage's manage page
- The system generates pairings automatically based on your configuration
- A pairing is where two groups face each other — that's where the actual matches take place
- Combined leaderboard across all pairings determines final standings

**Best for:** Final stages where you want the fairest possible evaluation across all remaining teams.

> **Example:** Grand Finals — 18 squads in 3 groups of 6. Group A vs Group B, Group A vs Group C, Group B vs Group C — 3 pairings total. Combined leaderboard determines the champion.

## Mixing Formats

Different stages in the same tournament can use different formats. This is how major events like FFIC and FFWS operate.

> **Example tournament:**
> - **Stage 1: Open Qualifiers** — Elimination, 192 squads in 16 groups of 12, top 4 advance
> - **Stage 2: Semifinals** — Elimination, 64 squads in 8 groups of 8, top 3 advance
> - **Stage 3: Grand Finals** — Round Robin, 24 squads in 4 groups of 6, pairings determine the champion

## Publishing Your Roadmap

Once your stages are configured, click **Publish**. You can edit the roadmap before publishing, but once published the stage structure is set.

After publishing, each stage shows a **Manage** button to access group creation, match management, and more.

## Roadmap Poster

You can turn your roadmap into a shareable poster image. Choose from 4 poster templates — Neon, Nebula, Inferno, or Phantom — and download a high-resolution PNG to share in your WhatsApp and Discord groups.

> **Tip:** Share the roadmap poster when you announce your tournament. Players want to see the full journey — how many stages, how teams advance, and what the finals look like.
