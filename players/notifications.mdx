The Notifications page collects all your platform activity in one place. Access it from the bell icon in the navigation.

Notifications arrive instantly — you don't need to refresh the page.

## Notification Tabs

- **All** — every notification you've received
- **Unread** — only notifications you haven't opened yet
- **Saved** — notifications you've bookmarked for later reference

## What You Get Notified About

- **Match scheduling** — when your matches get a time or are rescheduled
- **Results** — when match results are posted or updated
- **Room details** — when room ID and password are shared for your matches
- **Announcements** — when an organizer posts updates for your group
- **Group assignments** — when you're assigned to a group in a tournament stage
- **Stage completion** — when a stage ends and qualification results are published
- **Account security** — password changes, two-factor authentication updates, passkey activity

## Actions

- **Mark all as read** — clear all unread notifications at once
- **Save** — bookmark important notifications for later
- **Open** — click any notification to go directly to the relevant page
- Notifications are automatically marked as seen when they scroll into view

## Customizing Notifications

From **Settings → Notifications**, you can control exactly what you receive:

- Turn specific notification types on or off
- Set preferences per tournament or per group — so you only get what matters to you
- Changes take effect immediately

> **Tip:** If you're in multiple tournaments, customize notifications per tournament to avoid getting overwhelmed. Keep results and room details on, turn off what you don't need.
