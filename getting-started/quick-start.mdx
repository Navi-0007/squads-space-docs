This guide walks you through creating and running your first event on SquadsSpace.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

Head to [platform.squads-space.com](https://platform.squads-space.com/auth) and sign up with your email and password. Everyone starts as a player.

## Step 2: Unlock Organizer Tools

Open a ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/ySAsxatCwv) and we'll activate your organizer account. Once activated, you'll have access to both your Player Dashboard and Organizer Dashboard.

> **Tip:** While waiting for activation, explore the platform as a player — browse tournaments, set up your squad, and get familiar with the experience your players will have.

## Step 3: Create a Tournament or Scrim

From the Organizer Dashboard sidebar, go to **Tournaments** and click **Create**.

You'll walk through a creation flow:

1. **Name, description, and poster** — give your event an identity
2. **Visibility and registration mode** — Public (Open or Approval Required) or Private (Invite Only or Invite with Approval)
3. **Game mode** — Solo, Duo, or Squad
4. **Slots** — how many teams can register
5. **Schedule** — registration open, registration close, tournament start, and tournament end
6. **Requirements** — toggle what players need to meet:
   - Registering user must join club
   - Require full roster — all squad slots must be filled
   - Require all members in club (auto-enables the first two)
   - Require UID/IGN — players provide their Free Fire User ID and In-Game Name for this tournament
7. **Communication links** — your WhatsApp group and Discord server
8. **Point table** — set kill points and placement points per position

Not sure whether to create a Scrim or Tournament? See [Scrims vs Tournaments](/getting-started/scrims-vs-tournaments).

> **Tip:** Start with a scrim if you're new to the platform. It's simpler — one group, fixed slots, no roadmap. Great for testing your workflow before running a full tournament.

> **Tip:** Enabling "Require UID/IGN" means players provide their Free Fire ID for this specific tournament. This powers features like per-player kill tracking and Smart Results. You can enable it with or without requiring a full roster.

## Step 4: Build Your Roadmap (Tournaments Only)

After creating a tournament, build your roadmap. Define each stage — how many slots, how many teams advance, whether there are invited slots, and whether it's Elimination or Round Robin. The system calculates everything automatically.

Once your roadmap is set, it's locked. You can download it as a shareable image using one of the free templates, or as a PDF — share it in your WhatsApp and Discord groups so players can see the full journey.

Scrims don't have roadmaps — they're single-group events.

## Step 5: Share and Collect Registrations

Publish your tournament. Players can find it by browsing the platform or through a direct link you share.

For private events, share the secure invite link. You can regenerate it anytime if it gets leaked.

If you chose Approval Required, review incoming registrations from your dashboard — approve or reject each one individually or use bulk actions.

> **Tip:** Share your tournament link in your WhatsApp and Discord groups with the registration deadline. Players register faster when there's a clear cutoff.

## Step 6: Set Up Groups

Once registrations are in, set up groups for each stage. Configure group size and how invited slots are distributed (spread across groups or in a dedicated group). Click **Generate** and the platform creates all groups and assigns teams.

## Step 7: Schedule Matches and Share Room Credentials

Set the schedule for each match. Enter the room ID and password — players see them directly on the platform under their Match Hub. No need to broadcast credentials in WhatsApp or Discord.

> **Tip:** You can schedule all matches at once using mass operations — set date, time, room ID, and password for multiple matches in one go.

## Step 8: Enter Scores

After each match, you have two options:

**Smart Results (recommended for speed):**
1. Take a screenshot of the in-game results screen
2. Upload it from the match page
3. The system reads the scores automatically
4. Review, adjust if needed, and confirm

**Manual entry:**
1. Open the match from your dashboard
2. Enter each team's kills and placement in the score table
3. If UID/IGN is enabled, you can also enter per-player kills
4. Click **Save**

Points are calculated automatically using your point table. The leaderboard updates instantly.

> **Tip:** Smart Results works best with clear, full-screen screenshots. If UID/IGN is enabled for the tournament, the system can match scores to individual players automatically.

## Step 9: Share Results

Your leaderboard updates live as you enter scores. Share the link in your groups — players can check standings anytime.

If you're streaming, add your overlay URL as a browser source in OBS. SquadsSpace offers two overlay styles:

- **Side overlay** — a compact leaderboard that sits alongside your game feed. Use it during live matches.
- **Full-screen overlay (16:9)** — a detailed standings view that fills the entire screen. Use it between matches or after results are in.

Both update live as you enter scores — no need to refresh or re-add the source.
