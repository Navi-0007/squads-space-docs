Any player on SquadsSpace can become an organizer. The process is simple — open a ticket on Discord and we'll get you set up.

## Before You Start

You need a SquadsSpace account first. If you don't have one, [sign up here](https://platform.squads-space.com/auth).

## How to Apply

1. Visit the [Become Organizer](https://platform.squads-space.com/organizer/register) page on the platform to see what's included
2. When you're ready, open a ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/ySAsxatCwv)
3. Share your account email and club name in the ticket
4. Our team activates your account — usually within a few hours

> **Tip:** Have your club name ready before opening the ticket. This is what players see when they browse your tournaments — pick something recognizable to your community.

## What You Get

Once activated, your account is upgraded instantly. You'll have:

- **Organizer Dashboard** — your home base for managing tournaments, scrims, and your club
- **Tournament creation** — full access to create and manage events
- **Club management** — customize your club with a banner, social links, and member controls
- **Stream overlays** — broadcast-ready leaderboards for your streams
- **Smart Results** — upload screenshots to enter scores automatically

Your player features remain fully available — you can still browse and register for other organizers' tournaments.

## Your First Steps After Activation

1. **Customize your club** — add a banner, social links, and description in Club Settings
2. **Explore the dashboard** — check out the sidebar for Tournaments, Club, and Settings
3. **Create your first event** — follow the [Organizer Quick Start](/getting-started/quick-start) to run your first tournament or scrim

> **Tip:** Join our [Discord community](https://discord.gg/XjQf9nXQ6S) for organizer tips, support, and to connect with other tournament hosts.
