This guide gets you from zero to registered for your first tournament in minutes.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

Head to [platform.squads-space.com](https://platform.squads-space.com/auth) and sign up with your email and password. That's it — you're in.

## Step 2: Set Up Your Squad

You can create a Global Squad and invite your teammates. This makes registering for any tournament quick and easy — anyone from the squad can register the team. You only set it up once, and it works across all events.

If you don't have a Global Squad, no worries — you can form a Custom Squad when registering for a specific tournament.

> **Tip:** Setting up a Global Squad first saves time. You won't have to invite teammates every time you register for a new event.

## Step 3: Browse Tournaments

From your Player Dashboard, browse open tournaments and scrims. You can see event details like format, game mode, schedule, and available slots.

You can also join directly from a link an organizer shared in WhatsApp or Discord.

> **Tip:** Check the registration mode before registering. "Open" means you're confirmed instantly. "Approval Required" means the organizer reviews your registration first.

## Step 4: Register Your Team

When you find an event, you have two ways to register:

- **Global Squad** — register with your existing squad. Select which members to include (e.g., pick at least 4 for Squad mode if full roster is required). Fast and simple since your team is already set up.
- **Custom Squad** — form a new team just for this event. Create the squad, then share the invite link with your teammates. They accept, and they're in.

## Step 5: Meet the Requirements

Some tournaments have requirements set by the organizer. You'll see exactly what's needed on your Registration Status page:

- **Club membership** — join the organizer's club. If all members are required to join, your teammates will see a banner letting them know.
- **Full roster** — your team must have the minimum number of members before registration is complete. Share the invite link with teammates to fill remaining spots.
- **UID/IGN** — provide your Free Fire User ID and In-Game Name for this tournament. The person who registered the team can submit UIDs for all members, or each member can provide their own from the Registration Status page.

Each requirement updates automatically as conditions are met — no need to refresh.

## Step 6: Invited Slots

Sometimes an organizer may invite you directly to a specific stage using an invite link. When you receive one, you can register in two ways:

- **Global Squad** — register with your existing squad, same as regular registration
- **Solo** — register alone and provide your IGN and UID if required

If the tournament uses "Approval Required", the organizer still needs to approve your registration even with an invite link.

## Step 7: Get Assigned and Play

Once your registration is confirmed, you'll be assigned to a group. From your **Match Hub**, you can see:

- Your match schedule with dates and times
- Room ID and password for each match — right on the platform, no hunting through WhatsApp or Discord
- Match status (Scheduled, Live, or Completed)

> **Tip:** Bookmark your Match Hub page on match day for quick access to room credentials.

## Step 8: Check Results

After matches, view live leaderboards that update as the organizer enters scores. Check your match history to see how you've performed across events.

You'll also receive notifications when results are posted, matches are scheduled, or room details are shared. Customize what you receive from **Settings → Notifications**.
