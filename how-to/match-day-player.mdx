It's match day. This guide walks you through everything you need — where to find your room ID, how to check your schedule, and how to follow standings.

## Before Match Day

Make sure you've completed everything on your Registration Status page:

<Screenshot src={ss_player_registration_status} alt="Registration Status page" caption="Check that all requirements are complete — club membership, roster, UID/IGN" />

- **Registration confirmed** — your status shows "Confirmed"
- **Requirements met** — all requirement checkmarks are green
- **Group assigned** — you've been placed in a group (check your Match Hub)

> **Tip:** Complete your requirements the day before match day. Don't wait until the last minute — if something goes wrong (like a teammate not joining the club), you'll need time to fix it.

## Finding Your Match Hub

Your Match Hub is where everything happens on match day. Access it from:

- **My Tournaments** on your Player Dashboard → click the tournament card
- **The tournament page** → "Matches" link appears when you're an active participant

<Screenshot src={ss_player_match_hub} alt="Match Hub" caption="Your Match Hub — schedule, room credentials, standings, all in one place" />

## Getting Room Credentials

Room ID and password appear in your Match Hub when the organizer starts the match (status changes to **Live**).

1. Open your Match Hub
2. Select the current match from the match selector
3. Look for the **Room ID & Password** card
4. Copy the room ID and password
5. Enter them in Free Fire to join the custom room

> **Tip:** Room credentials appear as soon as the match goes Live. Keep your Match Hub open in your mobile browser — when the organizer starts the match, refresh once and you'll see the credentials immediately.

## Checking Your Schedule

The Match Hub shows your full match schedule:

- **Match selector** — switch between Match 1, Match 2, etc.
- **Schedule card** — date, time, status, and map for each match
- **Status indicators** — Not Scheduled, Scheduled (with time), Live (green pulsing), or Completed

## Following Standings

### During the Tournament

The **Standings** tab shows your group's cumulative leaderboard across all completed matches:

- Team rankings with total points, kills, and placement points
- **Qualifier line** — highlighted threshold showing how many teams advance
- Updates after each match result is posted

> **Tip:** Check where you stand relative to the qualifier line. If you're below it, you know how many points you need to climb in remaining matches.

### During a Live Stream

If the organizer is streaming with live scoring, the **Live Scoring** tab shows standings updating in real time as scores are entered:

- **Live Stream** mode — watch scores change as the organizer enters them
- **Match snapshots** — see individual match results

## Notifications

You'll receive notifications for key events:

<Screenshot src={ss_player_notifications} alt="Notifications" caption="Get notified when matches are scheduled, results are posted, or room details are shared" />

- **Match scheduled** — when your match gets a time
- **Room details shared** — when room ID and password are available
- **Results posted** — when match results are uploaded
- **Announcements** — when the organizer posts updates for your group

### Customizing Notifications

From **Settings → Notifications**, control what you receive:

- Toggle notification types on or off
- Set preferences per tournament or per group
- Turn off what you don't need to avoid notification overload

> **Tip:** For match day, make sure "Room details" and "Match scheduled" notifications are on. These are the ones you need most.

## Match Day Checklist

- [ ] Registration confirmed and all requirements complete
- [ ] Group assigned — check Match Hub
- [ ] Match schedule noted — know when your matches start
- [ ] Notifications on — especially room details and scheduling
- [ ] Match Hub bookmarked on mobile for quick access
- [ ] Room credentials ready — refresh Match Hub when match goes Live
- [ ] Free Fire updated and ready to launch

## Troubleshooting

**"I can't see room credentials"**
- Room ID and password only appear when the match status is **Live**. Check the match status indicator — if it still says "Scheduled", the organizer hasn't started it yet.

**"I'm not assigned to a group"**
- The organizer creates groups after registration closes. If you see "Group assignment pending", wait for the organizer to set up groups.

**"My requirements aren't complete"**
- Go to your Registration Status page and check which requirements need action. Common issues: teammate hasn't joined the club, roster not full, UID not submitted.
