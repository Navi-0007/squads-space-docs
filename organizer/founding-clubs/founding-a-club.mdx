When you become an organizer, you create a club. Your club is your identity on SquadsSpace — it's how players find you, join your community, and compete in your events.

## What Is a Club?

A club is your organizer profile on the platform. It includes your club name, branding, and a public page where players can see your tournaments, members, and community links.

## What Your Club Gives You

- **Public club page** — players can browse your profile, see your tournaments, and join
- **Member management** — view members who've joined your club
- **Tournament branding** — all your tournaments and scrims are associated with your club
- **Club membership requirement** — use "Require Club Membership" or "Require All Members in Club" in your tournaments to grow your community through registrations
- **Community growth** — every tournament you run with club requirements brings new members

## How Players Join

Players can join your club in several ways:

- Browsing and joining from your public club page
- Being prompted to join during tournament registration (when club membership is required)
- Seeing the join requirement on a tournament Registration Status page

Joining is free and instant. Players can be members of multiple clubs.

> **Tip:** Enable "Require Club Membership" on your tournaments to grow your club organically. Every player who registers joins your club — building your community for future events.

## Getting Started

Your club is created when you become an organizer. To get set up, open a ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/ySAsxatCwv) — see [Becoming an Organizer](/getting-started/becoming-an-organizer) for the full process.
