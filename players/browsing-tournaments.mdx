## All Tournaments

The All Tournaments page shows every public tournament and scrim on SquadsSpace. You can switch between grid and list views.

{/* TODO: screenshot — All Tournaments page */}

### Filters

Narrow down tournaments using:

- **Search** — find by name
- **Format** — Solo, Duo, or Squad
- **Mode** — Battle Royale or Clash Squad
- **Status** — Upcoming (published but not yet open) or Registration Open (currently accepting players)
- **Sort** — Relevance, Newest, or Starting Soon

### Tournament Cards

Each card shows the tournament name, organizer club, format, status badge, poster image, and registration progress (how many slots are filled vs total capacity).

> **Tip:** Look for the status badge on each card. "Registration Open" means you can register right now. "Draft" and "Published" tournaments aren't accepting registrations yet.

## My Tournaments

Once you've registered for tournaments, they appear in My Tournaments. This page shows all your active and past registrations — use the status filter to find specific ones.

## Club Pages

You can also discover tournaments through club pages. Every organizer has a public club page showing all their events. Browse clubs from the Clubs directory.

> **Tip:** Found an organizer you like? Join their club to easily find their future events from your dashboard.
