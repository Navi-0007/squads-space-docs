## Finding the Register Button

From any tournament page, look for the Join button in the hero banner. The button label changes depending on the situation — "Register", "Join", or "Apply" for approval-required tournaments. If the tournament is private, you'll need an invite link from the organizer to access it.

## Registration Flow

### Solo Tournaments

For Solo format, registration is straightforward — you register as an individual player. No team selection needed.

### Duo and Squad Tournaments

For team formats, you'll choose how to register:

**Global Squad** — register with your existing squad. Your squad roster is used as-is. This is the fastest option if your team is already set up.

**Custom Members** — build a custom team for this specific tournament. Pick a squad name and select which members to include. Useful when you want a different lineup than your permanent squad.

After choosing, you may need to select specific members (for Custom) or confirm your roster.

> **Tip:** If you register with a Global Squad, any member of the squad can be the one to register the team — it doesn't have to be any specific person.

### Invite Link

For private tournaments (Invite Only or Invite with Approval), the organizer shares an invite link with you. Click the link and it takes you directly to the registration page. Without an invite link, private tournaments won't show a register option.

### Club Membership

If the organizer requires club membership, you'll be prompted to join their club during registration. This is a one-click action right in the registration flow.

## After Registering

What happens next depends on the registration mode:

- **Open** — you're confirmed immediately
- **Approval Required** — your registration shows as "Needs Approval". The organizer reviews and approves or rejects it.
- **Invite Only** — confirmed immediately (you already had an invite)
- **Invite with Approval** — shows as "Needs Approval" for organizer review

After registering, you'll see a success screen with next steps — like inviting teammates, providing your UID/IGN, or waiting for approval.

You'll also get a registration invite link to share with teammates who need to join your team.

> **Tip:** Share the registration invite link in your WhatsApp group so teammates can join quickly — they just click and accept.

## Invited Slots

If an organizer gives you an invited slot link for a later stage (like semifinals or finals), you can register through that link directly. Invited slot registration lets you join the specific stage the organizer designated.

## UID/IGN

If the tournament requires UID/IGN, you'll need to provide your Free Fire User ID and In-Game Name. Depending on how the organizer set it up:

- The person who registered the team can submit UIDs for all members
- Or each member can provide their own from the Registration Status page

You can load your UID from your profile if you've saved it before, or enter a new one for this tournament.

> **Tip:** Double-check your UID before submitting — it's used for per-player kill tracking and Smart Results. An incorrect UID means your kills won't be matched to you.
