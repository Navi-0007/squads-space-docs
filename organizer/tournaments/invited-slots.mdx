Invited slots let you bring teams directly into any stage of your tournament, bypassing earlier rounds. This is how major events like FFIC and FFWS operate — pro teams get direct entry to later stages.

## How It Works

When you configure your roadmap, you specify how many invited slots a stage has. The system reserves those slots separately from the teams advancing through earlier stages.

## Managing Invited Slots

Each stage with invited slots has a dedicated Invited Slots page. From here you can:

- **Copy the invite link** — a secure link you share with the teams you want to invite
- **Rotate Token** — if the link is active and gets leaked, rotate to invalidate the old link and generate a new one
- **Regenerate Link** — create a fresh invite link if the current one is inactive
- **Revoke Link** — permanently deactivate the current invite link
- **Track capacity** — see how many invited slots are filled vs remaining
- **View joined squads** — see which teams have accepted with their team name, registration date, group assignment, and status

### Actions on Joined Teams

For each team that joined through the invite link, you can:

- **Approve** or **Reject** — if the tournament uses Approval Required mode
- **View Members** — see the team's roster details
- **Remove** — remove a team from the invited slots (requires confirmation)

## How Players Register via Invite Link

When a player receives your invite link, they have two ways to register:

- **Global Squad** — register with their existing squad on the platform, same as regular registration
- **Solo registration** — register alone and provide their IGN and UID if required by the tournament

## Approval and Invite Links

If your tournament uses Approval Required mode, invited registrations still need your approval before they're confirmed. Invited teams appear as "Pending" in the joined squads list. You approve or reject them from the action menu.

For Open registration tournaments, invited teams are confirmed automatically when they register through the link.

## The Invite Flow

1. Open the stage's Invited Slots page
2. Copy the invite link (or create one if none exists)
3. Share it with the teams you want to invite (WhatsApp, Discord, DM, etc.)
4. Invited teams use the link to register (Global Squad or solo)
5. If Approval Required, approve their registration from the joined squads list
6. When you create groups, invited teams are distributed based on your invited slots handling strategy (distribute evenly, concentrate, or seed into first groups)

> **Tip:** Rotate the token instead of revoking the link if you just want to block the old URL. Rotating creates a new link instantly while keeping the invite batch active.

## When to Use Invited Slots

- **Rewarding past champions** — give last season's winners direct entry to later rounds
- **Pro-am events** — mix professional teams with community qualifiers
- **Sponsor obligations** — guarantee spots for specific teams
- **Balancing competition** — ensure strong teams are spread across the tournament

> **Example:** Phoenix Cup — Stage 1: Open Qualifiers (192 squads, Elimination). Stage 2: Grand Finals (24 squads, Round Robin, 6 invited slots). You share the invite link with 6 pro squads. When you create groups for Grand Finals, the system places those 6 invited squads based on your handling strategy, and fills the remaining 18 slots with teams advancing from Qualifiers.
