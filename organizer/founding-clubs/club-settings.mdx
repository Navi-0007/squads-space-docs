Club Settings lets you shape how your club appears to players across the platform. Access it from the Organizer sidebar under **Club Settings**.

{/* TODO: screenshot — Club Settings page */}

## Club Identity

- **Club name** — your club's display name. Appears on your public club page, tournament cards, and everywhere your club is referenced.
- **Region** — your country or region. Helps players find local organizers when browsing clubs.

## Social Links

Add social links so players can connect with your community outside of SquadsSpace:

- **Discord** — your server invite link
- **YouTube** — your channel URL
- **WhatsApp** — your group or community link

Social links appear on your public club page and in the Organizer Dashboard.

> **Tip:** Add your Discord server link — it's where most Free Fire communities coordinate. Players check your social links when deciding whether to join your tournaments.

## Club Banner

Upload a banner image that appears at the top of your public club page. A good banner helps your club stand out when players are browsing.

> **Tip:** Use your club logo or a branded graphic. The banner is the first thing players see on your club page — make it count.
