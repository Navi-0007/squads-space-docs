This guide walks you through building a complete tournament roadmap — from planning your stages to sharing the roadmap poster with your community.

## Before You Start

You need a tournament created first (in Draft or Published status). If you haven't created one yet, see [Creating a Tournament](/organizer/tournaments/creating-a-tournament).

## Step 1: Open the Roadmap Builder

From your Tournament Dashboard, go to the **Roadmap** tab. Click **Configure** to open the builder.

<Screenshot src={ss_roadmap_builder_empty} alt="Empty roadmap state" caption="The Roadmap tab before configuration — click Configure to start" />

## Step 2: Plan Your Stages

Before building, plan your tournament structure. Ask yourself:

- **How many stages?** (e.g., Qualifiers → Semifinals → Finals)
- **How many teams per stage?** (first stage = total registrations, later stages = qualifiers + invited)
- **Elimination or Round Robin for each stage?**
- **Any invited slots?** (pro teams, defending champions, wildcards)

> **Example: Phoenix Cup**
> - Stage 1: Open Qualifiers — 192 squads, Elimination, top 4 per group advance
> - Stage 2: Semifinals — 64 squads, Elimination, top 3 per group advance
> - Stage 3: Grand Finals — 24 squads, Round Robin, 6 invited pro teams

## Step 3: Add Your First Stage

For each stage, configure:

| Field | What it means | Example |
|-------|--------------|---------|
| **Entrants** | Total teams in this stage | 192 |
| **Invited Slots** | Extra teams invited directly (on top of qualifiers) | 0 for first stage |
| **Group Size** | Teams per group (based on game mode) | 12 for Squad |
| **Qualifiers per Group** | How many advance from each group | 4 |
| **Matches per Group** | How many matches each group plays | 4 |

The system auto-calculates group count based on entrants and group size.

<Screenshot src={ss_roadmap_builder_stage_config} alt="Stage configuration" caption="Configuring a stage — entrants, group size, qualifiers, and matches" />

## Step 4: Add More Stages

Click to add the next stage. For stages after the first:

- **Entrants** auto-fills based on how many teams qualify from the previous stage + invited slots
- Set the invited slots if you want to add directly-invited teams to this stage
- Choose **Elimination** or **Round Robin** — you can mix formats across stages

> **Tip:** Use Elimination for early stages (narrows the field fast) and Round Robin for the final stage (fairest evaluation across all remaining teams).

## Step 5: Check the Final Stage

For the last stage, check the **Final Round** checkbox. This:

- Limits the stage to 1 group
- Asks for a winner count (how many teams win prizes)
- Marks this as the tournament's conclusion

<Screenshot src={ss_roadmap_builder_stages} alt="Complete roadmap with all stages" caption="A complete 3-stage roadmap — qualifiers, semifinals, finals" />

## Step 6: Publish the Roadmap

Review your stages, then click **Publish**. Once published:

- The stage structure is set
- Each stage shows a **Manage** button for group creation and match management
- You can still edit stage names from Stage Settings

> **Tip:** Double-check your qualifiers-per-group numbers before publishing. After publishing, you can't change the stage structure — only stage names.

<Screenshot src={ss_roadmap_builder_published} alt="Published roadmap" caption="Published roadmap — each stage now has a Manage button" />

## Step 7: Share the Roadmap Poster

Turn your roadmap into a shareable image:

1. From the Roadmap tab, open the poster creator
2. Choose from 4 poster templates: **Neon**, **Nebula**, **Inferno**, or **Phantom**
3. Preview how your roadmap looks
4. Download the high-resolution PNG

Share the poster in your WhatsApp and Discord groups so players can see the full tournament journey before registering.

<Screenshot src={ss_roadmap_poster_templates} alt="Roadmap poster templates" caption="Choose from 4 poster templates — Neon, Nebula, Inferno, Phantom" />

> **Tip:** Post the roadmap poster alongside your tournament announcement. Players are more likely to register when they can see the full journey — qualifiers through finals.

## Common Roadmap Structures

### Small Tournament (24-48 teams)
- **Stage 1:** Qualifiers — 2-4 groups, Elimination, top 6 advance
- **Stage 2:** Finals — 1 group, Round Robin, winner takes all

### Medium Tournament (96-192 teams)
- **Stage 1:** Open Qualifiers — 8-16 groups, Elimination, top 4 advance
- **Stage 2:** Semifinals — 4-8 groups, Elimination, top 3 advance
- **Stage 3:** Grand Finals — Round Robin with 6 invited pro teams

### Large Tournament (200+ teams)
- **Stage 1:** Open Qualifiers — 16+ groups, Elimination, top 4 advance
- **Stage 2:** Closed Qualifiers — 8 groups, Elimination, top 3 advance
- **Stage 3:** Semifinals — 4 groups, Elimination, top 3 advance
- **Stage 4:** Grand Finals — Round Robin with invited slots
