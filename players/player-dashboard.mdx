The Dashboard is the first thing you see after signing in. It gives you a snapshot of everything happening across your tournaments, clubs, and squad.

{/* TODO: screenshot — Player Dashboard */}

## Quick Actions

Shortcut buttons at the top for the most common tasks — browse tournaments, manage your squad, view your clubs, and more.

## My Tournaments

Shows your active tournament registrations. Click any card to jump to that tournament's Match Hub or Registration Status page.

> **Tip:** If a tournament shows a requirement badge (like "UID needed" or "Join club"), tap the card to go straight to the Registration Status page and complete it.

## Clubs

If you've joined any clubs, they appear here with quick links to each club's page. If you haven't joined any yet, you'll see top clubs on the platform to explore.

## Recommended Tournaments

Tournaments you might be interested in — based on what's currently open for registration.

## Organizer Events

If you're also an organizer, a dedicated section shows tournaments you manage — keeping your player and organizer activities separate but accessible from the same dashboard.
