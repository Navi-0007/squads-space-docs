SquadsSpace is a tournament platform built for Free Fire in India. Create tournaments, manage registrations, enter scores, and broadcast results — all in one place. Your players get room credentials, live leaderboards, and instant notifications without leaving the platform.

## For Organizers

SquadsSpace replaces the Excel sheets, calculators, and manual work behind your tournaments. Your community stays on WhatsApp and Discord — SquadsSpace is the engine behind the scenes.

What you can do:

- **Create tournaments and scrims** with custom formats, point tables, and registration settings
- **Build multi-stage roadmaps** — qualifiers → semifinals → finals with elimination and round robin formats
- **Manage registrations** — 4 modes: open, approval required, invite only, or invite with approval
- **Enter match scores** and get instant point calculations — no more manual math
- **Use Smart Results** — upload a screenshot of the results screen and the system reads the scores for you
- **Stream with live overlays** — broadcast-ready scoreboards that update as you enter scores
- **Auto-generate slot lists** for the next round based on results
- **Track per-player kills** tied to verified Free Fire UIDs
- **Share room credentials** — players see the room ID and password directly on the platform

> **Tip:** You can use Smart Results (upload a screenshot) or enter scores manually — whichever is faster for your workflow.

## For Players

SquadsSpace is where you find and join Free Fire tournaments and scrims. No more scrolling through WhatsApp groups for room IDs or asking "bhai score kab aayega?"

What you can do:

- **Browse tournaments and scrims** — discover events, check formats, and see what's open
- **Register your team** — fill in your details, track your registration status, and get confirmed
- **Get room ID & password** — see your match credentials right on the platform, no Discord hunting
- **View match schedules** — know exactly when your matches are happening
- **Get notified instantly** — receive notifications when matches are scheduled, results are posted, room details are shared, or announcements go out. Customize what you receive per tournament, per group, or globally from Settings
- **View live leaderboards** — watch standings update as scores are entered
- **Check your match history** — see your past results across events

> **Tip:** SquadsSpace works great on mobile browsers — no app download needed. Bookmark it for quick access on match day.

## Getting Started

**Organizers:** Sign up at [platform.squads-space.com](https://platform.squads-space.com/auth), then open a ticket on [Discord](https://discord.gg/ySAsxatCwv) to unlock organizer tools. Check the [Organizer Quick Start](/getting-started/quick-start) to see what's possible.

**Players:** Sign up at [platform.squads-space.com](https://platform.squads-space.com/auth) and browse tournaments immediately. See the [Player Quick Start](/getting-started/quick-start-player) to register for your first event.
