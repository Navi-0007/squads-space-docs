The Tournament Dashboard is where you manage everything about a specific tournament. It opens when you click on a tournament from your Organizer Dashboard.

## Quick Actions

At the top of the dashboard, you have quick action buttons:

- **Status** — open Status Control to manage tournament lifecycle
- **Share** — get your tournament's public link and share via WhatsApp, Discord, or Email
- **Edit** — open the tournament editor
- **Registrations** — jump to registration management
- **Delete** — delete the tournament (requires confirmation)

> **Tip:** Use the Share button to quickly copy your tournament link and post it in your community groups.

## Dashboard Tabs

### Overview

Shows the tournament's current status, key stats (registrations, capacity), and a summary view. From here you can publish, access status control, and navigate to stages.

For completed, cancelled, or paused tournaments, the overview shows a dedicated view with the relevant information and available actions (like resuming a paused tournament).

### Roadmap

View your tournament's stage structure. If you haven't created a roadmap yet, you'll see a prompt to configure one. Once created, each stage card links to the stage management page. You can also download the roadmap as an image or PDF.

### Registrations

View all registrations with filtering, search, and bulk actions.

Filter by status:
- **All** — every registration
- **Pending** — waiting for your approval
- **Confirmed** — approved and counted toward slots
- **Rejected** — registrations you've rejected

See [Reviewing Registrations](/organizer/managing-players/reviewing-registrations) for details.

### Game Settings

Shows your tournament's scoring configuration at a glance — points per kill, placement points table, tiebreaker priority, allowed platforms, and team size limits. These are set during tournament creation and can be edited from the Edit page.

### Analytics

Registration progress, confirmed count, and capacity metrics. Charts for detailed analytics are coming in a future update.

## Sharing

The Share modal lets you:

- Copy your tournament's public link
- Share directly to WhatsApp, Discord, or Email
- Regenerate the server invite link if the old one gets leaked
