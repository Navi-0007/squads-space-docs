SquadsSpace generates groups automatically based on your configuration. You set the rules — the platform builds the groups and assigns teams.

## How to Access

From your stage's manage page, go to **Groups**. If no groups exist yet, click **Configure auto-create** to open the settings.

## Configuration Options

### 1. Group Naming

Choose how your groups are named:

- **Auto-generate names** — set a prefix (e.g., "Group") and a pattern (Letters A-Z or Numbers 1-9). Result: "Group A", "Group B", etc.
- **Custom names** — name each group manually. Useful when you want names like "Warriors Pool", "Legends Pool", etc.

### 2. Invited Slots Handling

If your stage has invited slots, choose how invited teams are distributed across groups:

- **Distribute evenly** — spread invited squads across all groups to avoid stacking advantages.
- **Concentrate** — pack invited squads into as few early groups as possible. Requires enough invited slots to fill at least one full group.
- **Seed into first groups** — place invited squads into the earliest groups first before filling later groups.

If the stage has no invited slots, this setting is ignored.

### 3. Seeding Strategy

How registered teams are distributed across groups:

- **Balanced random** — shuffle entrants while keeping group sizes even. This is the default and works for most events.
- **Rank-based** — distribute teams by skill tiers (coming soon).

### 4. Fill Strategy

How remaining slots are filled after invited teams are placed:

- **Balance across all groups** — distribute participants evenly so every group stays roughly the same size.
- **Fill one group at a time** — fill each group to capacity before moving on to the next one.

## Creating Groups

Once your settings are configured:

1. Click **Preview Groups** to see how teams would be distributed
2. Review the preview — check group sizes and team assignments
3. Click **Create Groups** to confirm and build the groups

> **Tip:** Always preview before creating. The preview lets you catch any issues with group balance or invited slot distribution before committing.

## After Groups Are Created

Once groups exist, you can:

- **View all groups** and their team assignments in expandable cards
- **Move teams between groups** — select teams and use Multi Swap view to redistribute
- **Manage invited slots** — if your stage has invited slots, there's a separate Invited Slots page. See [Invited Slots](/organizer/tournaments/invited-slots) for details.

## Auto-Assign

Auto-assign is an optional feature you can enable during group configuration. When turned on, the system automatically distributes new registrations into groups every 15 minutes, using your fill and seeding strategies.

This is useful for large tournaments where registrations come in over days or weeks — groups stay balanced without you manually assigning anyone. You can see when auto-assign last ran from the settings page.

You can still move teams between groups even with auto-assign enabled.

## Locking Groups

Before you can create matches, you must lock the groups. Locking means:

- No more moving or rearranging teams between groups
- Auto-assign stops running
- Group composition is finalized
- You can now create matches for each group

To lock, use the lock controls on the Groups page. You'll see each group's lock status — a green indicator means locked, amber means unlocked.

To make changes after locking, use the **Unlock Groups** button at the top of the page. Only groups whose matches haven't started can be unlocked.

> **Tip:** Make sure invited slots are filled and teams are where you want them before locking. Review your groups one final time, then lock and move on to scheduling matches.
