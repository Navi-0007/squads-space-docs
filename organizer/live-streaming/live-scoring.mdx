Live Scoring lets you enter match results while streaming. As you input scores, the overlay updates instantly — your viewers see standings change live.

## Starting a Session

From any group or pairing page, go to **Live Scoring**. You'll see a setup screen before starting:

1. **Session info** — teams count, match count, and player tracking stats (how many teams have IGNs)
2. **Edit Slots** — review and adjust the team/player roster before going live
3. **Configure broadcast** — set up your overlay display (tournament name, stage name, handles, templates). See [Stream Overlays](/organizer/live-streaming/stream-overlays) for details.
4. Click **Start Live Scoring** — a confirmation asks if you're ready to begin

Once started, the **OBS Overlay URLs** modal appears with your overlay links. Copy them and add to OBS as browser sources.

> **Tip:** Configure your broadcast details and set up OBS browser sources before clicking Start. Once live, you want to focus on scoring, not setup.

## Entering Scores Live

During a live session, you work through matches one at a time:

1. Select the current match from the match tabs at the top
2. Enter placement and kills for each team as results come in
3. Click **Push Scores** to update the overlay — viewers see standings change instantly
4. Move to the next match when ready

The bottom bar shows: **End Session** (to stop) and **Push Scores** (to broadcast current scores).

> **Tip:** Use Team Kills mode for speed during live broadcasts. Player Kills mode is more detailed but takes longer to enter — better for post-match entry.

## During the Session

- **Push Scores** sends your current entries to the overlay. Push as often as you want — viewers see updates instantly.
- If you switch matches with unsaved changes, you'll be prompted to push first or switch without pushing.
- The connection indicator shows whether the overlay is receiving your updates.

## Ending a Session

Click **End Session** when you're done:

- If you have unsaved changes, you'll be warned
- If not all matches are scored, you'll see how many are pending
- The session ends and overlay viewers are disconnected

After ending, you can submit all scored results at once, or discard and go back.
